# Settlers of Natac

The fun alternative to Colonist, which is the alternative to Settlers of Catan. Play solo with bots or multiplayer with friends. No download or sign-up required!

**[Play at natac.natetan.com](https://natac.natetan.com)**

## Maps

Classic and Random, plus these custom shapes:

- Random+ · procedurally generated compact islands
- Random++ · wild islands with branching peninsulas and deep bays
- Star
- Crescent
- Long Island
- China

All maps support standard (3–4 players) and large (5–6 players) sizes.
